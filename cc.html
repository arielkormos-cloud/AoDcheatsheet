Turning-point plan

TP1
Build the castle without becoming static
  • Straddle your home objective and a mid-board route with the matrix.
  • Stage Crawlers where TP2 3APL activations matter.
  • Keep Reanimator difficult to reach; its survival is often worth more than a weak shot.
  • Use Warriors as the disposable edge of the formation.
TP2
Explode out of the matrix
  • Use 3APL to combine attack + mission action + reposition.
  • Amplification is your reliability spike.
  • Cranial Overload can ruin a key enemy activation.
  • Move nodes only when the new matrix immediately improves scoring or attack angles.
TP3
Trade expendables, preserve engines
  • Warriors can die for points; Geomancer/Reanimator usually should not.
  • Use Animate to shift the battlefield rather than dragging the entire team.
  • Force opponents to fight inside your preferred matrix lane.
TP4
Break the castle if scoring demands it
  • Late game, the matrix is a tool—not the objective.
  • Spend Warriors/Crawlers to deny or steal mission points.
  • Don't preserve pieces that can score now.

1. 🏃 Mobile / evasive teams

Warrior bias
2× Gauss • 1× Tesla

Key tools

Animate NodesRepulsionCranial OverloadAwakened Nodes

Plan

  • Do not chase them with the whole castle. Make one lane extremely expensive and score elsewhere with Warriors.
  • Move the matrix proactively to cut off their next objective route.
  • APL reduction is especially valuable on operatives that need move + mission + escape.
  • Community lesson: opponents often beat CC by refusing the matrix. Your answer is to make the matrix cover scoring space, not empty space.

2. 🔫 Shooting teams

Warrior bias
2× Gauss • 1× Tesla

Key tools

HypershieldingShield FlarePhase ShifterNanoscarab Caskets

Plan

  • Keep the matrix intervening between firing lanes and Crawlers whenever possible.
  • Do not expose Reanimator to the same Blast/Torrent attack as the model it may need to save.
  • Use a Crawler as the visible threat while the rest of the team scores behind it.
  • Save Shield Flare for a damage instance that changes a breakpoint, not the first chip damage.

3. ⚔️ Melee teams

Warrior bias
2× Gauss • 1× Tesla

Key tools

RepulsionSouldrainSacrificial ThrallReanimate

Plan

  • Use the matrix as a movement tax so charges fail or become awkward.
  • Souldrain changes melee breakpoints; fight inside the matrix whenever you can.
  • Screen the Geomancer/Reanimator with expendable Warriors rather than trying to win every melee.
  • Keep a route for a Reanimated operative's free Dash back into safety.

4. 🛡️ Defensive / bunker teams

Warrior bias
2× Gauss • 1× Tesla

Key tools

AmplificationAnimate NodesIsolator Crawler

Plan

  • Don't feed Crawlers into prepared guns. Force the bunker to react to matrix repositioning and objective pressure.
  • Use the Isolator against expensive models where a successful Banishment swing matters.
  • Take ground with Warriors while Crawlers punish anything that leaves cover.
  • If they refuse to move, win on mission instead of searching for kills.

5. 👥 Horde teams

Warrior bias
1× Gauss • 2× Tesla

Key tools

AmplificationAnimate NodesAccurate 1

Plan

  • Tesla living-lightning profiles punish clustering around objectives.
  • Don't waste Crawlers overkilling 7-wound bodies unless it opens a scoring lane.
  • Use your 3APL Macrocytes to keep pace with their action economy.
  • Force multiple cheap enemies to spend actions killing a Warrior that already scored.

6. 💀 Elite teams

Warrior bias
3× Gauss or 2× Gauss + 1× Tesla

Key tools

AmplificationIsolator + Gauss CrawlersOverchargeNanoscarabs

Plan

  • Focus one elite operative until it is removed; spreading damage is usually a mistake.
  • Accurate 1 + Ceaseless makes the Crawlers dramatically more reliable.
  • The Isolator is your high-variance execution threat; soften the target before the Banishment roll.
  • Your numerical advantage matters. Make their 5–6 models solve too many scoring problems.
