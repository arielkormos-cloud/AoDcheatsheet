1. 🏃 Mobile teams

Team
Captain • Grenadier • Intercessor Gunner • 3× Assault Warrior

Equipment

Purity Seals Tilting Shields Stun Grenades Terrain piece / barricade

Ploys / Doctrine

Tactical Doctrine Assault Doctrine Shock Assault Wrath of Vengeance

Plan

  • Pressure objectives instead of trying to out-angle a highly mobile team.
  • Use Assault Warriors to chase, screen and contest.
  • Stun can wreck the activation of an objective runner.
  • Use Wrath whenever the opponent’s model count gives you useful counteracts.

2. 🔫 Shooting teams

Team
Captain • Grenadier • Intercessor Gunner • Eliminator • 2× Assault Warrior

Equipment

Purity Seals Auspex 2× Smoke Portable / Heavy Barricade

Ploys / Doctrine

Devastator Doctrine Adjust Doctrine Tactical Doctrine Transhuman Physiology

Plan

  • Use Smoke to cross dangerous firing lanes.
  • Eliminator creates a threat lane while Assault Warriors close in.
  • Use Auspex when obscuring is protecting a key target.
  • Keep CP for Transhuman when losing the operative would really hurt.

3. ⚔️ Melee teams

Team
Captain • Grenadier • Intercessor Gunner • 3× Assault Warrior

Equipment

Purity Seals Tilting Shields Chapter Reliquaries Stun Smoke

Ploys / Doctrine

Assault Doctrine Shock Assault Wrath of Vengeance Transhuman Physiology

Plan

  • Do not let the enemy choose every engagement.
  • Counter-charge with Assault Warriors.
  • Captain is your central brawler / anchor.
  • Tilting Shields are especially valuable into crit-driven melee.
  • Use Shock Assault on the fight that really matters.

4. 🛡️ Defensive / bunker teams

Team
Captain • Grenadier • Gunner • Eliminator • Heavy Gunner • Assault Warrior

Equipment

Purity Seals Auspex Smoke Barricade / mobility equipment

Ploys / Doctrine

Devastator Doctrine Adjust Doctrine Tactical Doctrine

Plan

  • Bring the maximum specialist shooting package.
  • Make the opponent move rather than feeding models into their setup.
  • Use Auspex to attack defensive obscuring positions.
  • Switch to Tactical once the fight compresses into short range.

5. 👥 Horde teams

Team
Captain • Grenadier • Gunner • 3× Assault Warrior

Equipment

Purity Seals Chapter Reliquaries Tilting Shields Utility Grenades / board equipment

Ploys / Doctrine

Tactical Doctrine Assault Doctrine Wrath of Vengeance Shock Assault

Plan

  • Grenadier and Gunner’s frag launcher punish grouped operatives.
  • Expect frequent counteracts because you only have six bodies.
  • Wrath converts those counteracts into much better action economy.
  • Do not overinvest in huge guns just to kill cheap bodies.

6. 💀 Elite teams

Team
Captain • Grenadier • Intercessor Gunner • Eliminator • Heavy Gunner • Assault Warrior

Equipment

Purity Seals Tilting Shields Auspex Smoke Krak Grenades

Ploys / Doctrine

Devastator Doctrine Tactical Doctrine Assault Doctrine Transhuman Physiology Shock Assault

Plan

  • Focus damage and remove one enemy operative at a time.
  • Use Heavy + Eliminator to maximize quality shooting.
  • Save Transhuman for an operative whose survival changes the game.
  • Your Grenadier already has strong grenade access, so extra Krak is optional.
