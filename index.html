🎯 Shooting — quick check

Tap highlighted terms for the rule explanation
  1. Can the shooter see the target? Check Visibility.
  2. Is it a valid target? Check its Order, Cover, and Control Range.
  3. Is the target obscured? Check Obscured, including effects like Smoke.
  4. Do special rules change target selection? Check Vantage, Seek, and similar rules.
  5. Is the weapon in range? Check Range if the weapon has a limit.
  6. Roll attack dice. Use the weapon's Atk and Hit stats, then apply rules such as Accurate, Balanced, Lethal, Severe, etc.

🛡️ Defending against shooting

The basic defence sequence
  1. Collect defence dice. Normally you roll 3 defence dice. Piercing can reduce how many dice you get.
  2. Check cover before rolling. If you are in Cover, you can normally retain one defence die as a normal success without rolling it. Saturate stops retained cover saves.
  3. Roll the remaining defence dice. Results equal to or above your Save are successes. A 6 is a critical success; a 1 always fails.
  4. Use your saves to cancel attack dice. One normal save blocks one normal hit. One critical save blocks one normal or one critical hit. Two normal saves can block one critical hit.
  5. Take damage from anything left. Unblocked normal attack successes deal Normal Damage; unblocked critical successes deal Critical Damage.
