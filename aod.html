Turning-point plan

TP1
Stage safely and create TP2 threats
  • Do not expose a Marine just for a low-value opening shot.
  • Use Grenadier/Gunner and your Assault Warriors to threaten the centre from cover rather than occupying it too early.
  • Choose your first doctrine around the shots you will actually take: Devastator for meaningful long-range fire, otherwise save CP.
  • Keep at least one Assault Warrior positioned to punish an enemy that overextends onto a mid-board objective.
TP2
Commit where one activation changes the board
  • Use Combat Doctrine for the range band where most of the fighting will happen.
  • Grenadier/Gunner should soften or finish a priority target; Assault Warriors then contest, charge or screen.
  • Use Shock Assault only on a fight whose result matters for scoring or removes a dangerous operative.
  • Plan for counteracts: leave useful second actions available instead of ending every model in a dead position.
