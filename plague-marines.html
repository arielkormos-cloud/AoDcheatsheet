Equipment default

General default: Plague Bells + Poison Vents, then choose Plague Rounds or Blight Grenades by list/map.
  • Plague Rounds: higher value if Warrior/bolt weapons will shoot repeatedly.
  • Blight Grenades: higher value against hordes, dense maps and objective clustering.

Turning-point plan

TP1
Positioning is everything
  • Move 5 means a bad deployment can cost the whole game.
  • Stage Champion/Fighter for TP2 counter-charge, not a TP1 suicide rush.
  • Keep Plaguecaster central enough to heal and spread Poison.
TP2
Poison before the important interaction
  • Virulent Poison can switch on Contagion without first winning an attack.
  • Once poisoned, enemies entering your close zone become dramatically worse.
  • Use the Icon Bearer to make Contagion free once it reaches enemy territory.
TP3
Own the middle
  • This is where durability and healing compound.
  • Lumbering Death rewards shooting/fighting without moving more than 3 inches.
  • Make the enemy remove 14-wound Marines from scoring positions.
TP4
Ignore wounds, score points
  • Your surviving models are hard to finish and can still counteract.
  • Use late-game Astartes double actions to mission + reposition or attack twice where legal.

1. 🏃 Mobile teams

Suggested six
Champion • Fighter • Icon • Plaguecaster • Warrior • Heavy Gunner/Bombardier

Equipment

Plague BellsPlague RoundsPoison Vents

Ploys

ContagionNurglingsVirulent Poison

Plan

  • You cannot out-run them. Attack their APL and movement instead.
  • Poison a key runner, then Contagion can reduce movement while Nurglings reduces APL.
  • Warrior adds straightforward ranged presence so you do not have to chase every target.
  • Make them enter your 7-inch/3-inch control zones to score.

2. 🔫 Shooting teams

Suggested six
Champion • Icon • Plaguecaster • Warrior • Fighter • Heavy Gunner/Bombardier

Equipment

Plague BellsPlague RoundsPoison Vents

Ploys

Cloud of FliesSickening ResilienceLumbering DeathWarrior

Plan

  • Cloud of Flies gives a genuine protected staging point; use it on the model(s) that must cross a firing lane.
  • Warrior's Repulsive Fortitude is specifically attractive when the opponent relies on conventional shooting.
  • Do not walk every model through the same exposed lane. Advance in layers.
  • Sickening Resilience is best when it changes multiple damage dice in one activation.

3. ⚔️ Melee teams

Suggested six
Champion • Fighter • Icon • Plaguecaster • Bombardier • Heavy Gunner/Warrior

Equipment

Poison VentsPlague BellsBlight Grenades

Ploys

ContagionCurse of RotSickening ResilienceVirulent Poison

Plan

  • Do not be afraid of the centre. Melee enemies entering 3 inches are entering your best rules.
  • Poison Vents punishes repeated close activations, especially once Poison is already applied.
  • Contagion degrades enemy Hit and movement; Fighter/Champion then counter-charge.
  • Curse of Rot can turn the opponent's rolled 3s into damage + failed results.

4. 🛡️ Defensive / bunker teams

Suggested six
Champion • Icon • Plaguecaster • Warrior • Heavy Gunner • Bombardier/Fighter

Equipment

Plague RoundsPlague BellsPoison Vents

Ploys

Cloud of FliesLumbering DeathVirulent PoisonMiasma

Plan

  • Your weakness is reach. Don't spend the game trading weak long-range shots.
  • Use Cloud of Flies to establish a midfield foothold that forces the bunker to react.
  • Warrior gives the cleanest ordinary long-range gun; Plague Rounds increases its pressure.
  • Once within 7 inches, Plaguecaster turns the matchup into your game.

5. 👥 Horde teams

Suggested six
Champion • Bombardier • Heavy Gunner • Icon • Plaguecaster • Fighter

Equipment

Blight GrenadesPoison VentsPlague Bells

Ploys

Poisonous DemiseVirulent PoisonContagionPlague SpewerPlague Wind

Plan

  • This is where your area attacks earn their slots. Punish objectives with multiple bodies.
  • Poison on several cheap operatives adds up because each activation costs them damage.
  • Do not waste Fighter/Champion overkilling 7-wound models if an objective action is worth more.
  • Poisonous Demise can make killing one of your Marines expensive when enemies are clustered nearby.

6. 💀 Elite teams

Suggested six
Champion • Fighter • Icon • Plaguecaster • Warrior/Heavy Gunner • Bombardier

Equipment

Plague BellsPlague RoundsPoison Vents

Ploys

Lumbering DeathCurse of RotSickening ResilienceMiasmaVitality

Plan

  • Poison one elite early so every later rule becomes better.
  • Focus one enemy model until dead; your team is durable enough to survive a slower trading game.
  • Use Plaguecaster healing to erase chip damage and destroy enemy breakpoint planning.
  • Lumbering Death rewards holding ground and repeatedly attacking from the same scoring zone.

Four rules to remember

Poison
Apply it before the important attack whenever possible.
Contagion
Turns close range into a hostile zone.
Sickening Resilience
Best defensive spike when many damage dice are coming.
Putrescent Vitality
Huge healing can invalidate the opponent's previous activation.
