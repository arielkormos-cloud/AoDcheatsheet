Default game plan

  • TP1: advance into positions that threaten the centre on TP2. You are Move 5, so bad deployment is hard to repair.
  • TP2: start applying Poison before the decisive attacks so Toxic/Contagion effects matter.
  • TP3–4: stand on the important ground and force the opponent to remove models that are unusually inefficient to kill.
  • The Warrior is your swap-in when its straightforward boltgun/Toxic profile and Repulsive Fortitude are more valuable than a specialist.
