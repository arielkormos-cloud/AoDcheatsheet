1. Battle flow

Set up
Prepare the killzone and deploy.
  • Standard killzone is normally 30" × 22" unless the mission says otherwise.
  • Determine initiative and drop zones according to the mission.
  • Players alternate deploying roughly one third of their team at a time, starting with the player who has initiative.
  • Operatives begin wholly within their drop zone and normally start with a Conceal order.
Turning points
Normally 4 turning points.
  • Each turning point has a Strategy phase then a Firefight phase.
  • At the start of Strategy, determine initiative and ready operatives.
  • Players alternate strategic gambits/strategy ploys until both pass in succession.
  • In Firefight, players alternate activations, beginning with the player who has initiative.

Command points

  • Each player starts the battle with 2CP.
  • First Strategy phase: both players gain 1CP.
  • Later Strategy phases: the player with initiative gains 1CP; the player without initiative gains 2CP.
  • Strategy and firefight ploys normally cost 1CP unless stated otherwise.
  • Command Re-roll can re-roll one attack or defence die after rolling.
Activation
  1. Select a ready friendly operative.
  2. Choose its Engage or Conceal order.
  3. Perform actions. Each action spends AP; total AP spent cannot exceed the operative's current APL.
  4. Unless a rule says otherwise, the same action cannot normally be performed more than once during that activation.
  5. When finished, the operative becomes expended.
All your operatives expended first? You may get Counteract opportunities while the opponent still has ready operatives.

2. Orders, ready & expended

Engage order

Normal offensive order. The operative can normally Shoot and Charge, can be targeted if visible, and can counteract when eligible.

Conceal order

Defensive order. A Concealed operative in cover is normally not a valid target, but it normally cannot Shoot or Charge unless another rule permits it.

Ready
Can be activated.

Ready/expended is separate from Engage/Conceal.

Expended
Has already activated.

An expended Engage operative may later be chosen to counteract if the requirements are met.

4. Shoot sequence

  1. Select a ranged weapon. Apply restrictions such as Range x or Heavy.
  2. Select a valid target. Check Visible, order, Cover, Obscured, range and control-range restrictions.
  3. Roll attack dice. Roll the weapon's Atk characteristic. Results meeting Hit succeed; 6 is critical; 1 fails.
  4. Defender collects defence dice. Normally 3, modified by effects such as Piercing x.
  5. Cover save. If eligible, the defender may retain a defence die as a normal success without rolling it. Saturate stops this.
  6. Roll remaining defence dice. Results meeting Save succeed; 6 is critical; 1 fails.
  7. Block attack successes. One normal save blocks one normal hit; two normal saves can block one critical hit; one critical save blocks one normal or critical hit.
  8. Resolve unblocked attacks. Normal hits inflict Normal Dmg; critical hits inflict Critical Dmg.

5. Fight sequence

  1. The attacker selects an enemy operative within its control range. That enemy retaliates.
  2. Both players choose one melee weapon.
  3. Both roll attack dice. Results meeting Hit succeed; 6 is critical; 1 fails.
  4. Starting with the attacker, players alternate resolving one success at a time.
  5. Strike: inflict the selected weapon's normal or critical damage.
  6. Block: discard one opponent success. A normal blocks a normal; a critical blocks a normal or critical.
  7. If one player has no successes remaining, the other player can resolve all remaining successes.

6. Visibility, cover, obscured & valid targets

Visible

Visibility is literal line of sight from the viewing operative's head to the target model, ignoring bases.

Control range

Usually requires visibility and being within 1". Control range is mutual once established.

Cover

Intervening terrain close enough to the target can provide cover. An operative within 2" of the shooter cannot use cover against that shooter.

Obscured

A separate protection created by qualifying Heavy terrain/effects between attacker and target. Cover and obscured are not the same rule.

Fast valid-target test
  • Engage target: normally valid if visible and all other restrictions are satisfied.
  • Conceal target: normally valid only if visible and not in cover, unless a rule such as Seek/Vantage changes that.
  • A target cannot be in cover from and obscured by the same terrain feature in one shooting sequence. If both would apply from that feature, the defender chooses one.

7. Movement

  • Movement is measured by the actual path the operative's base travels.
  • An operative must finish a move where its base can legally be placed.
  • Reposition, Dash, Charge and Fall Back each have different control-range restrictions.
  • Climbing, dropping, jumping and Accessible terrain can consume movement allowance even when horizontal displacement is small.
  • Current commentary says combined vertical climbing distance is totalled before rounding. An operative can continue a climb onto the next terrain when the next section is within 1" horizontally and 3" vertically.
Charge
Move allowance = Move + 2".

Must finish in enemy control range. A Concealed operative normally cannot Charge.

Jump
From Vantage higher than 2".

Current errata allows a jump up to 4" horizontally from the edge in one straight-line increment, followed by any required drop/climb.

8. Terrain concepts

Light Heavy Vantage Accessible Insignificant Exposed Ceiling

Terrain can have more than one trait. Killzone-specific rules (Volkus, Gallowdark, Tomb World, Bheta-Decima, etc.) can add or replace interactions, so check the relevant killzone rules as well as the core rules.

9. Damage, wounds & injured

  • Damage reduces an operative's remaining wounds.
  • An operative below its starting wounds is wounded.
  • An operative below half its starting wounds is injured.
  • At 0 wounds or less it is incapacitated and removed after any permitted before-removal rules are resolved.
  • Current rules limit an incapacitated operative to at most one free action before removal, excluding Place Marker, when a rule permits such an action.

11. Current clarifications worth remembering

  • Counteract: current errata excludes Guard. The operative cannot move more than 2", and if a rule removes and sets it up again it must be set up within the permitted 2" counteract limit.
  • Cover + obscured: the same terrain feature cannot provide both in the same shooting sequence; the defender chooses one if both would otherwise apply.
  • Heavy weapons: movement restriction applies during an activation or counteraction.
  • Severe: a critical generated by Severe can enable Devastating and Piercing Crits, but does not enable Punishing or Rending.
  • Torrent: secondary targets must themselves be valid targets and cannot be within control range of friendly operatives.
  • Guard: in Close Quarters killzones, an operative that actually interrupts from Guard cannot also counteract later that turning point.
  • Precedence: when rules still conflict after higher-priority principles are applied, current errata places the active operative's controlling player ahead of the initiative player for the relevant tie-break.
