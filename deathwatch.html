Equipment default

Most generally useful: Bioscryer Cuffs + Servo-Thrall + matchup equipment. The Sergeant's flexible-equipment rule gives extra room.
  • Ammunition Reserve rises in value when you have two high-value shooting activations in one turning point.
  • Digital Weapons is especially useful when bringing multiple melee specialists.

Turning-point plan

TP1
Preserve all five
  • Do not give away a model for a weak early shot.
  • Set up future counteracts; you have fewer activations than almost everyone.
  • Use mission actions efficiently with Servo-Thrall rather than exposing a shooter.
TP2
Create one decisive overload
  • Pick the enemy order for Mission Tactics.
  • Use specialist + ammunition + counteract to remove one important model.
  • Do not spread damage across several enemies unless it scores immediately.
TP3
Exploit double-action counteracts
  • Auspicator Tracking can flip orders before counteracting.
  • Think in pairs: Dash + mission, Reposition + mission, Shoot + mission—not Fight + Shoot.
TP4
Spend bodies for points
  • Five operatives means surviving models are extremely action-dense.
  • Late game, trade a veteran if it converts directly into primary/secondary points.

1. 🏃 Mobile teams

Suggested five
Sergeant • Disruptor • Headtaker • Gunner • Blademaster/Aegis

Equipment

Servo-ThrallBioscryer CuffsAmmunition ReserveDigital Weapons

Ploys

Mission TacticsAuspicator TrackingTranshuman

Plan

  • Headtaker's mobility helps you threaten runners without moving the whole team.
  • Disruptor gives you a way to punish a key high-mobility activation.
  • Don't chase every flank. Lock down the objectives that force them to interact.
  • Counteracts are your answer to being out-activated—stage so your late actions still matter.

2. 🔫 Shooting teams

Suggested five
Sergeant • Aegis • Disruptor • Gunner • Headtaker/Blademaster

Equipment

Bioscryer CuffsServo-ThrallAmmunition ReserveDigital Weapons

Ploys

Long VigilTranshumanAuspex ScanMission Tactics

Plan

  • Do not mirror a superior gunline. Use Aegis and terrain to advance one safe layer at a time.
  • Advanced Auspex Scan punishes obscuring-based safety.
  • Headtaker is valuable if there is a route to cross the board from Conceal.
  • Long Vigil rewards keeping at least part of the battle in your territory until you are ready to commit.

3. ⚔️ Melee teams

Suggested five
Sergeant • Blademaster • Aegis • Headtaker • Gunner/Demolisher

Equipment

Digital WeaponsBioscryer CuffsServo-ThrallAmmunition Reserve

Ploys

Mission TacticsTranshumanAuspicator Tracking

Plan

  • Layer counter-charge threats so the opponent cannot isolate one veteran.
  • Blademaster is the default melee answer; Demolisher comes in when raw hammer damage matters more.
  • Remember Mission Tactics works on retaliations too.
  • Do not let the enemy charge your five models one at a time; overlap threat ranges.

4. 🛡️ Defensive / bunker teams

Suggested five
Sergeant • Disruptor • Gunner • Marksman • Headtaker/Aegis

Equipment

Ammunition ReserveServo-ThrallBioscryer CuffsDigital Weapons

Ploys

Advanced Auspex ScanMission TacticsLong Vigil

Plan

  • Marksman + Gunner force respect at range; Headtaker gives you the angle they cannot simply bunker against.
  • Use Servo-Thrall to score while the shooters remain in threatening positions.
  • Ammunition Reserve can create the one turning point where two shooters simultaneously punch through defences.
  • If the bunker refuses to move, take the board rather than forcing low-odds shots.

5. 👥 Horde teams

Suggested five
Sergeant • Horde-Slayer • Bombard/Breacher • Disruptor • Blademaster/Aegis

Equipment

Ammunition ReserveServo-ThrallDigital WeaponsBioscryer Cuffs

Ploys

Blast 1" SIAHorde-SlayerBombard

Plan

  • Bring at least one multi-target specialist. Horde-Slayer is the obvious choice if Torrent lanes exist.
  • Blast ammunition is strongest when it generates an extra meaningful attack, not just chip damage.
  • You cannot kill everything. Remove the operatives doing mission actions or threatening multiple points.
  • Your veterans' counteracts partly close the action-count gap—keep them alive long enough to use them.

6. 💀 Elite teams

Suggested five
Sergeant • Gunner • Blademaster • Demolisher/Disruptor • Aegis

Equipment

Digital WeaponsAmmunition ReserveBioscryer CuffsServo-Thrall

Ploys

Mission TacticsSuffer Not The AlienTranshumanLethal / Rending / Piercing Crits

Plan

  • Plasma Gunner is your cleanest ranged elite-killer.
  • Blademaster or Demolisher gives you a second premium removal tool.
  • Focus one elite until dead; a wounded enemy still contributes a full activation.
  • Against non-CHAOS/non-IMPERIUM elites, Suffer Not The Alien is a major reliability spike.
