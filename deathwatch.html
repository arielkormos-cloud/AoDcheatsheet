How to pilot

  • Do not trade 1-for-1 early. With only five bodies, an early loss hurts your scoring more than most teams.
  • Plan every turning point around counteracts. Veteran Astartes gives you two different counteract actions.
  • Auspicator Tracking lets you attack on Engage then counteract back onto Conceal, or approach concealed then flip to Engage.
  • Mission Tactics also works when retaliating: choose the order you expect to interact with most.
